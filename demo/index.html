Wearable MR guide: web stand-in

Gaze at the guide for the dwell time (four seconds) to start listening. If you stay silent, the guide greets you. Look at a flower and say “what is this” to recognise it, then ask follow-ups while looking at the guide. If you look away and stay silent, the conversation ends. With the mouse ray, the gaze stays where it was while you type or click outside the scene; point at empty space in the scene to look away.

Gaze: nothing

Object recognition source

Simulated rooms: each flower's label comes from the authored room description.

Spatial anchors: placed per room in advance; the first recognised flower loads that room's anchors and later questions about anchored flowers skip recognition. Persistent world-locked anchors are not reproduced in the web demo (WebXR Anchors exist only on Android/Quest browsers), so rooms and anchors are simulated here.
